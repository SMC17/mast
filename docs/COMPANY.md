# COMPANY — Sunlit Moon / mast

**Internal note. Not a public claim. Not a user-count receipt.
Do not syndicate. Do not paste into README, launch copy, or
fundraising decks.**

## Entity

Sunlit Moon. Product: mast. Repository is private; keep it private
until an operator explicitly opens it.

## ICP

Daily-driver holdouts: people who already refuse a wrapper IDE as the
editor of record and will keep a local kernel (Emacs, vim, Helix, or a
small scriptable binary) holding the buffers.

The ICP is *not* "Cursor users we will convert." That sentence is a
Type-I overclaim: mast has no G3 soak, no G4 independent review, and
no measured user base. "Cursor-killer" is marketing, not evidence.

## Path to 100 users

Hypothesis, not a count: reach 100 daily-driver holdouts through
Ziggit (and the Zig forum) issue-by-issue, PR-by-PR, not through VC.

- **What would confirm it:** 100 named operators, none of them the
  author, using mast as the daily editor for ≥30 days (G3-shaped
  evidence, just n=100).
- **What would refute it:** G3 still open, or the only "users" are
  the author and agents. A fundraise against an unmeasured 100 is
  itself a Type-I claim.
- **What we will not do:** VC before that evidence exists. Premature
  funding converts a kernel into a wrapper play.

100 users is a destination that G3 has to make possible. It is not a
shipped number, not a README line, and not a reason to open the repo.
